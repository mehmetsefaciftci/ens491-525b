## Simulation finding on asking vs inferring the preference

In 2,000 simulated visits the adaptive policy reaches 45.9% suitable formats when it infers the
preference from practice behaviour, 43.6% with no prior and 55.4% when it uses the participant's
stated pick (fixed comparator: 52.9%). Under these simulator assumptions, inferring from a short
practice is weak; the pilot measures how informative real practice behaviour is, and `?prior=stated`
lets the team compare both on real participants.

## From the literature to the implementation

| Source | What it measures and how | What we implement |
|---|---|---|
| Sweller 2020 [1]; Paas 1992 [14] | cognitive load; subjective effort ratings | brief/detailed by familiarity; Paas after every block; efficiency E with recall |
| Mayer 2009 [2] | retention and transfer tests; modality, redundancy, segmenting, spatial contiguity | recall + transfer items; audio without redundant text; segment-by-segment pacing; panel kept next to the painting |
| Stock et al. 2007 [3] | interest from implicit behaviour and explicit feedback | dwell, skips, replays; "Interesting" button as explicit evidence |
| Lindlbauer et al. 2019 [4] | optimisation of what to show and where in MR | utility-based format choice; optimised panel placement |
| He & Wu 2023 [5] | eye tracking, self-report and learning tests | recall safeguard; painting-in-view-while-listening proxy |
| Mehler et al. 2012 [7]; Fridman et al. 2018 [6]; Pluisch et al. 2025 [10] | controlled n-back load; participant-independent validation | 0-back/2-back calibration; LOSO evaluation of the load estimate (no physiological or face sensing) |
| Pfleging et al. 2016 [8]; Suzuki et al. 2024 [9] | pupil confounds; combine subjective, performance and objective measures | no pupil metrics; TLX + Paas (subjective), recall + time (performance), behavioural load (objective) |
| Hart 2006 [12]; Brooke 1996 [13] | Raw NASA-TLX; SUS | full 6-subscale Raw TLX; SUS per system |
| WCAG 2.2 [16] | captions, resize text, target size | transcript on demand, A−/A+ text size, 40 px+ targets |
| ISO 9241-11 [22] | effectiveness, efficiency, satisfaction | recall, interaction time, SUS |
